HOW I WORK
01—04

From first sketch
to finished detail.

01

Notice what matters

Start with the person, the task, and the moment where things get harder than they should.

02

Give it a point of view

Choose a visual language that fits the audience. Build a system without sanding off its character.

03

Make the thing work

Use HTML, CSS, and JavaScript as design tools. Prototype the real interaction, including its edge cases.

04

Use AI with judgment

Explore and implement faster with AI in the loop; keep the decisions, quality checks, and final craft human-led.

A PRACTICAL AI WORKFLOW

Fast drafts.
Careful decisions.

For this independent portfolio, I used ChatGPT to help scaffold the case-study model and wire repetitive interaction states. I then reviewed the content and visual hierarchy, refined the art direction project by project, and checked the keyboard paths, responsive rules, and edge states before publishing. AI helps me move through the first draft; design judgment stays with me.
